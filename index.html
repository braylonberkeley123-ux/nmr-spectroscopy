Why Spectroscopy Matters

Medicine

MRI scanners used in hospitals run on the same underlying physics as NMR spectroscopy — nuclear spin and radiofrequency resonance — just scaled up to image the human body instead of a test tube.

Pharmaceuticals

Drug developers rely on NMR to confirm a new compound's exact structure and purity before it moves forward — a mislabeled or contaminated molecule can derail years of research.

Space & Earth Science

Spectroscopy lets scientists identify minerals and compounds in samples they can't physically test in a lab — from ocean sediment cores to rock samples on Mars.
